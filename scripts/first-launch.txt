Opening Jarvis for the first time
=================================

1. Drag Jarvis into the Applications folder shown beside it, then open
   Applications and double-click Jarvis.

2. macOS will say it cannot verify the app. That is expected: this copy was
   built for personal use and is not signed with an Apple Developer ID.

   macOS 15 or later:
     Click Done on the warning. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security,
     scroll to "Jarvis was blocked", click Open Anyway, and confirm.

   macOS 14:
     Right-click (or Control-click) Jarvis in Applications, choose Open,
     then click Open in the dialog.

   You only do this once.

3. Jarvis opens a short setup. It needs one thing to start answering: a
   Vercel AI Gateway key. Create one at https://vercel.com/ai-gateway and
   paste it into the setup screen. The key is stored in your Keychain.

Good to know
------------
- Jarvis includes everything it needs. There is nothing else to install.
- Your conversations, memory and settings stay in
  ~/Library/Application Support/JarvisRoy on this Mac.
- After you replace Jarvis with a newer copy, macOS may ask again for
  Keychain, microphone or other permissions. That is a side effect of the
  app not being signed with a Developer ID.
- If Jarvis cannot start, the banner at the top of its window says why and
  has a "Copy diagnostics" button.

Only open a copy of Jarvis that you built or downloaded from your own
repository, and check it against the .sha256 file published beside it.
