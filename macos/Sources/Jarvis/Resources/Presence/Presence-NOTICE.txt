Jarvis presence (particle face)
===============================

presence-data.js and presence-engine.js are derived from the "AURA" standalone
demo supplied by the project owner.

Kept unchanged from that demo: the facial motion director and the WebGL 2
particle renderer. Removed: its voice engine, which loaded an ElevenLabs
client from the network and held its own conversation, and its page
interface. Changed: the canvas is transparent so the face can sit inside the
HUD core. Added: a small driver that only follows the voice state and the
measured audio levels pushed in by the app. Nothing in these files can open a
microphone, a socket or a URL, and the page that hosts them forbids all
network access.

Face geometry
-------------
The demo's manifest records the geometry as derived from
  https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r184/examples/models/gltf/facecap.glb
which three.js credits as "model by Face Cap" (https://www.bannaflak.com/face-cap).
The manifest refers to THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md and FaceCap-LICENSE.txt, which
were NOT included with the demo that was supplied.

The licence terms of that model have therefore not been verified here.
Confirm them, and add the missing notices, before distributing a build of
Jarvis that contains these files to anyone else.
